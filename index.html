What Predicts Bouncing Back from Mental Health Struggles?

Used mixed-effects models on UK Household Longitudinal Study data from 2010 to 2024, tracking GHQ-12 scores for over 13,000 struggling adults over two-year intervals. Sleep duration, vegetable intake, walking, and supportive relationships predicted mental health recovery; moderate or vigorous exercise did not do so significantly.

coauthored with Ian Kim (Project Lead), Allen Chen, Aparna Petluri and Nicole Chan
